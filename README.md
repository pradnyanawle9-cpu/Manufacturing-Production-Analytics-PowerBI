## 📈 Strategic Project Focus
The core objective of this industrial intelligence asset is to transform complex, multi-factory operational log files into an integrated performance cockpit. This platform allows plant managers and supply chain operations teams to audit machine reliability constraints, track shifts causing product defects, and control overall equipment maintenance budgets with zero reporting lag.

### 🌟 High-Impact Operational Insights & Core Features:
*   **Executive Throughput Analytics:** Structured real-time KPI metrics tracking **Total Output (23K Units Produced)** against a quality assurance ledger isolating **269 Defective Units**.
*   **Asset Reliability & Maintenance Budgeting:** Formulated horizontal bar matrices mapping total operational overhead across machinery, tracking an aggregate **131K Maintenance Cost** led by critical failures in **CNC Machine M1** and **Printer Assembly M4**.
*   **Shift Velocity Distributions:** Engineered shift breakdown metrics capturing high-volume execution during **Day Shifts (52.35%)** compared to specialized **Night Cycles (30.27%)**.
*   **Time-Series Productivity Horizons:** Integrated continuous tracking graphs evaluating **Average Production Hours (7.98 Hours)** to detect sudden bottlenecks or runtime drops across calendar weeks.
*   **Multi-Factory Quality Auditing:** Clustered performance charts across geographical entities (Nova Electronics plants) to isolate which factory infrastructure requires immediate quality control auditing.

## 🛠️ Data Architecture & Operational Processing Lifecycle
To maintain rapid cross-filtering across multi-factory assets, the project follows strict engineering standards inside Power BI:


[Raw Industrial Plant Logs (CSV)]
                  │
                  ▼
   [Power Query ETL Processing]      ──► (Null Auditing & Structural Type Conversions)
                  │
                  ▼
    [Relational Schema Modeling]     ──► (Establishing Lean Filtering Directions)
                  │
                  ▼
   [Advanced Analytics Visuals]      ──► (Dashboard Deployment & KPI Analytics)


1.  **Ingestion & Data Extraction:** Connected raw flat-files to Power BI Desktop, running data profiling inside Power Query to eliminate corrupt parameters and properly capture continuous floating metrics (like production hours).
2.  **Relational Modeling:** Deployed a high-performance relational schema, reducing record complexity to maintain smooth interactive metrics across factory and shift slicers.
3.  **Visual Metric Engineering:** Implemented strict visual rules using conditional formatting and high-contrast styling to immediately alert plant stakeholders to high defect rates or equipment failures.
